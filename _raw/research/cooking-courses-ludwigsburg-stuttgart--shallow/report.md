# Upcoming cooking courses near Ludwigsburg and Stuttgart

## Conclusion

For Wok technique, pick the VHS Stuttgart Thai course (21 September) or Vietnamese course (2 October): both explicitly cook in a wok and cost €59/€58. The most focused option is Kochschule Stuttgart's **Lass Wokken** on 3 November (€129), with wok tips and technique in a 3.5–4-hour four-course session.

Best general beginner option: VHS Stuttgart's four-evening **Grundkurs Kochen**, starting 22 September. For a single evening, VHS also has Thai cuisine (21 September), fermentation (24 September), Maultaschen (26 September), and everyday cooking (28 September). Kochschule Stuttgart offers more polished 3.5–4-hour evening menus, from €129.

Direct Ludwigsburg-area options were scarcer: Katsumoto in Bietigheim lists sushi courses, but its near-term September/October dates showed as fully booked in search results. Its 15 and 22 November dates showed three places remaining; confirm live before booking.

## Evidence

- **VHS Stuttgart, Rotebühlplatz:** Grundkurs Kochen, 22 September–27 October 2026, four Tuesday evenings, 18:00–22:00, €230; cooking fundamentals and techniques.
- **VHS Stuttgart:** Thai cuisine, 21 September, 17:45–21:45, €59; sustainable regional/vegan cooking, 23 September, €53; fermentation, 24 September, €59; Maultaschen, 26 September, €48; everyday cooking for beginners, 28 September, €52.
- **VHS Stuttgart, Wok-relevant:** Thai cuisine, 21 September at Bildungshaus NeckarPark, explicitly includes wok dishes (€59); Vietnamese cuisine, 2 October at Rotebühlplatz, dishes are prepared in the wok (€58); another Thai course runs 9 October (€58).
- **Kochschule Stuttgart, Wok-focused:** Lass Wokken, 3 November at 18:30, €129. It promises wok tips, tricks, and flavour techniques; includes a 4-course meal and drinks and runs about 3.5–4 hours.
- **Kochschule Stuttgart:** Basic – Einfach + Lecker, 23 September at 18:30, €129. Its course description says 3.5–4 hours, 4-course meal, drinks, equipment, and professional instruction included. October programme includes vegetarian (8 October), Asia (14 October), Mediterranean (20 October), Spanish (21 October), and fish/shellfish (28 October).
- **Katsumoto, Bietigheim bei Ludwigsburg:** sushi courses. Search listing marked 27 September, 11 and 25 October, and 1 November fully booked; 15 and 22 November had three spaces. Live course availability was not visible on the retrieved page.

## Trade-offs and risks

- Provider availability changes quickly. Confirm places, total price, dietary accommodations, and exact venue before paying.
- VHS is best value and skills-focused; private cookery schools are shorter, social/menu-led, and cost more.
- Search result for Vorwerk Ludwigsburg showed September/October Thermomix cooking shows, but opening its page returned an outdated May schedule. Excluded as unverified.

## Open questions

- Preferred date, cuisine, budget, and whether a multi-week basics course is suitable.
- Vegetarian/vegan, couples, or a specific technique such as sushi, baking, or grilling.
