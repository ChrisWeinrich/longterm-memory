# Deep research query

Research this question: Welche Grundlagen, Quellen und Bücher vermitteln einem
akademisch geübten Einsteiger zuverlässig, wie Finanzmärkte funktionieren –
einschließlich Aktien, Fonds/ETFs, Bondmarkt, Zinsen und Risiko – ohne eine
persönliche Anlageempfehlung zu erteilen?

Use the accepted outline as the scope. Prefer official investor education from
regulators and central banks, then original academic or publisher sources for
books; use reputable secondary sources only for context. Record URLs,
publication dates when available, evidence, uncertainty and inference
separately. Do not turn general educational material into an individualized
recommendation.

Explain the bond market carefully: issuer and investor roles; coupon, maturity,
yield and credit risk; inverse price/yield relationship; duration; yield curve;
and the limits of these simplified models. Distinguish long-term diversified
investing from active trading and make no profitability claims.

For books, identify enduring reference works and accessible entry points.
For each, state the documented edition/author when available, its purpose,
likely prerequisites, and an explicitly labelled assessment of reading
difficulty for an academic non-specialist. Provide a staged reading order.

Write the completed report to
`_raw/research/personal-finance-market-foundations/report.md` from
`_templates/raw-research-report.md`. It is Raw material without a state and is
curated into `wiki/pages/` later.
