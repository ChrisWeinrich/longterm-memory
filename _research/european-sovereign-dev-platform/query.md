# Deep research query

Research this question: Welche europäisch souveräne Plattform oder Kombination
ersetzt GitHub für private Infrastruktur-Repositories, Issues, einfache
Roadmaps und CI/CD?

Use the accepted outline as the scope. Prefer official provider, legal,
privacy, DPA/AVV, infrastructure and product documentation; then primary
software-project documentation; then reputable secondary context. For each
candidate verify separately: legal entity and jurisdiction, controlling company
where disclosed, processing/storage locations, subprocessors and transfer
language, repository/issues/roadmap/CI capabilities, portability, and limits.
Treat "EU region" as insufficient when the provider or contractual control is
non-European. Record source URLs, publication dates when available, evidence,
uncertainty, and inference separately.

Write the completed report to
`_raw/research/european-sovereign-dev-platform/report.md` from
`_templates/raw-research-report.md`. It is Raw material without a state and is
curated into `wiki/pages/` later.
