# Deep research query

Research this question: Wie funktioniert das Geld- und Finanzsystem in einer
modernen, kreditbasierten Wirtschaft – insbesondere im Euroraum – und welche
Begriffe und Bücher bilden dafür einen angemessenen theoretischen Einstieg?

Use the accepted outline as the scope. Prefer official educational material
from the Deutsche Bundesbank, ECB and financial regulators, then university
presses or publishers for books. Record URLs, dates where available, evidence,
uncertainty and inference separately. Explain the difference between an
educational simplification and a balance-sheet claim.

Cover money creation and destruction in bank lending, the relation of central
bank and commercial-bank money, the policy transmission mechanism, and the
primary/secondary market distinction. Explain bonds and equities as financing
claims, without transitioning to investment selection or practical advice.

For books, provide a staged, theory-first reading path and an explicit,
reader-specific difficulty assessment. Do not recommend products, strategies
or actions for personal wealth management.

Write the completed report to
`_raw/research/financial-system-foundations/report.md` from
`_templates/raw-research-report.md`. It is Raw material without a state and
is curated into `wiki/pages/` later.
